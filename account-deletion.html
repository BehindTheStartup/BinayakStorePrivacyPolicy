Account & Data Deletion Request

Request deletion of your account and associated data from Binayak's apps — no login or app install required.

What gets deleted

When your request is processed, we permanently delete:

  • Your profile information (name, phone number, email)
  • Your booking and order history
  • Saved preferences and app settings
  • Authentication credentials tied to your account

Some information may be retained where required by law (e.g. transaction records for tax/accounting purposes) for a limited period, after which it is also deleted.

Submit a request

This opens your email client with a pre-filled deletion request. We typically process requests within 30 days and will confirm by email once complete.
