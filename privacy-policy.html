Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 26, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Binayak Admin mobile application (the "App"), designed for back-office staff and administrators of Binayak Store to manage bookings, customers, inventory, and store operations.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Employee credentials (email, password)
  • Full name and employee ID
  • Role and permission level
  • Contact information (phone number)

1.2 Usage Data

  • Login activity and session duration
  • Features accessed within the app
  • Booking and customer management actions performed
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • App version and build number

1.3 Customer Data (Accessed Through the App)

  • Customer profiles, membership tiers, and booking history
  • Product catalogue and inventory information
  • Booking details, delivery addresses, and payment records
  • Staff communications and internal announcements

1.4 Technical Data

  • API request logs and error reports
  • Push notification tokens (FCM)
  • Network connection status
  • Crash reports and diagnostics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for:

  • Authentication and Access Control: Verifying employee identity and enforcing role-based permissions
  • Business Operations: Managing bookings, inventory, customers, and store settings
  • Communication: Sending notifications about bookings, announcements, and system updates
  • Analytics and Improvement: Understanding app usage patterns to improve features and performance
  • Security: Detecting unauthorized access, fraud, and security incidents
  • Compliance: Meeting legal and regulatory obligations

3. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between the app and our servers uses TLS/SSL encryption
  • Secure Storage: Authentication tokens are stored in device keychain/secure storage
  • Access Controls: Role-based permissions limit data access to authorized personnel only
  • Session Management: Automatic logout after inactivity; expired sessions require re-authentication
  • Server Security: Data is hosted on secure servers with regular security updates and monitoring

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data in the following circumstances:

  • Within Organization: With authorized Binayak Store staff members based on role permissions
  • Service Providers: With third-party services that support app functionality (hosting, push notifications, analytics)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, court order, or government regulation
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with advance notice)

5. Third-Party Services

The app integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): For push notifications
  • API Backend: Our server infrastructure for data synchronization

These services have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing them.

6. Data Retention

  • Active Employee Data: Retained while you remain employed and have an active account
  • After Account Termination: Personal data deleted within 90 days unless retention is required for legal, accounting, or security purposes
  • Audit Logs: Business transaction logs may be retained longer for compliance and financial record-keeping

7. Your Rights

As an employee user of this app, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal information stored in the app
  • Correction: Update inaccurate or incomplete information through your profile
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Restriction: Request limitation of processing in certain circumstances
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format

To exercise these rights, contact your administrator or reach out to us directly (see Contact section below).

8. Children's Privacy

This app is intended exclusively for employees aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. For material changes, we will notify users through in-app announcements or email.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed and stored in Nepal or other jurisdictions where our service providers operate. By using the app, you consent to the transfer of your information to these locations.

11. Your Consent

By using the Binayak Admin app, you consent to the collection, use, and sharing of your information as described in this privacy policy.
